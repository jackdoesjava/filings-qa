# Retrieval

Generated by scripts/04_retrieval_eval.py. 150 questions; 41525 chunks (overlapping windows of up to 200 words that never cross a page, 177 words on average) from 84 filings, between 7 and 1565 chunks per filing (median 447).

A hit at k means at least one of the top k chunks is from a gold evidence page. k counts chunks, so k = 5 is about 883 words, the amount of context the RAG answerer gets. MRR is the mean of 1/rank of the first chunk from a gold page, 0 if none comes back. Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over questions (2,000 resamples); differences are paired, using the same resampled questions for both methods.

Each question searches only its own filing, as in the FinanceBench paper's single vector store, and BM25 takes its statistics (idf, average chunk length) from that filing alone. The random row is what a random ordering of the filing's chunks would get on average.
