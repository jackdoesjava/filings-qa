# Page alignment

Generated by scripts/02_ingest.py.

FinanceBench gives each evidence item a page number (`evidence_page_num`) and the text of that page as they extracted it (`evidence_text_full_page`). Retrieval is scored against those page numbers, so before using them I compared every evidence page with our pymupdf parse at `evidence_page_num + offset` for offsets -1, 0 and +1: 189 evidence items from 150 questions, 168 distinct pages in 84 filings.

Overlap is a weighted Jaccard over word counts (lowercase letters and digits), and the same over character 5-grams once spaces and punctuation are removed. The second one decides: a page matches if it scores at least 0.8. On some financial statement pages FinanceBench's text has the spaces between words dropped ("Cashandcashequivalents"), and there the numbers are about the only words the two texts share.
